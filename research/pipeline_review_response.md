# Response to Claude’s pipeline review

[Verbatim review](claude_pipeline_result.md); request/response/report hashes are in [provenance](pipeline_claude_provenance.json). The independent numerical audit found no implementation discrepancy beyond1.60e-10. We accept the substantive challenge: passing this conservative, strong-signal screen validates integration, not scientific advantage.

**Changes accepted:** the result now includes the valid fixed-full baseline in the recall table and distinguishes pooled term recall from means of dataset ratios. Fixed full confirms56/115 weak quadratic terms versus45/115 for independent refit, while the latter wins weak affine88/180 versus60/180. Weak-term loss motivates a known-noise exact coefficient-test baseline. Its statistic is `z_j = beta_hat_C,j / [sigma sqrt((X_C'X_C)^(-1)_jj)]` under the full-family zero-coefficient null; two-sided tests need multiplicity control (e.g. six-test Bonferroni). This tests a coefficient in the supplied full family, not the accuracy of the frozen candidate's coefficient. Do not silently substitute one claim for the other or assume universal power dominance.

**Claims qualified:** conservatism does not establish that the engineering guard was impossible to fail. The above-nominal Q counts are correlated and small; they do not establish miscalibration. Independent matrix recomputation verifies code, not empirical calibration. A future calibration diagnostic must be preregistered rather than repeatedly sampling until rates look reassuring. The current data do not separately identify conservatism versus refit/extrapolation error as the cause of lost power. Nor is one fixed optimal design established for every proposed discrimination objective.

**Fission is an option, not free data.** [Rasines and Young](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.02159) and [Leiner et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.11079) describe randomized alternatives to splitting (primary abstracts checked September26). For `tau > 0`, Gaussian `Y` with covarianceΣ and independent `Z~N(0,Σ)`, our direct covariance calculation gives independent `Y+tau Z` and `Y-Z/tau`, with variances `(1+tau²)Σ` and `(1+tau⁻²)Σ`. Both retain sites while dividing information. It neither duplicates information nor automatically supplies three independent selection/refit/audit streams; the new covariance and testing target need their own derivation. Defer that extension.

**Next decision:** avoid another isolated active-design demonstration with obviously wrong functions. Review one small reject → revise → fresh-check loop across graded misspecification, with exact coefficient-test and always-large/ordinary-selection baselines. Predeclare operators, revision budget, independent confirmation, and sequential error control; evaluate whether revision improves common-query prediction and explanation without excessive false revisions. The supplied grammar remains an assumption, so this would still be a controlled known-method study. Active design can follow inside that loop if a defensible comparison remains. No new experiment was run in response to this critique.
