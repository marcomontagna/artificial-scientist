# Closest reference points

These sources establish overlap, not novelty clearance. Primary abstract records checked September 26, 2026; no complete literature review or reproduced-paper claim.

- [Learning to Perform Physics Experiments via Deep Reinforcement Learning — Denil et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.01843): agents interact with physical scenes to infer properties. Experiments as actions are established prior art; our first toolbox does not require an RL policy.
- [Toward an AI Physicist for Unsupervised Learning — Wu and Tegmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.10525): learning/manipulating predictive theories and their applicability across physics environments overlaps directly with the long-term goal.
- [WorldCoder — Tang, Key and Ellis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.12275): an LLM agent builds executable world models through interaction. This is a close reference for model programs, exploration and planning; our zero-extra-spending local prototype does not assume paid model calls.
