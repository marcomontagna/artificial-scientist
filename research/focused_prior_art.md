# Focused novelty check — 2026-09-25

Author: Codex primary. Complements the initial map and the independent critic's controlled-sensing review. This is a bounded decision review, not an exhaustive literature survey. Primary pages were accessed today; no paper experiments were reproduced.

## What changes the decision

- **Prediction-Oriented Bayesian Active Learning**, Freddie Bickford Smith, Andreas Kirsch, Sebastian Farquhar, Yarin Gal, Adam Foster, Tom Rainforth, AISTATS 2023. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v206/bickfordsmith23a.html), [author-hosted paper](https://sebastianfarquhar.com/assets/papers/bickfordsmithPrediction2023.pdf). Abstract and introduction read. EPIG already targets predictive information under a chosen target-input distribution, whereas parameter information can prioritize irrelevant inputs. Therefore “learn useful information, not all information” is not our new principle. It supplies a relevant comparator, not evidence it remains superior under hidden temporal shifts.
- **Characterizing the robustness of Bayesian adaptive experimental designs to active learning bias**, Sabina J. Sloman, Daniel M. Oppenheimer, Stephen B. Broomell, Cosma Rohilla Shalizi, 2022 preprint. [Record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.13698), [text](https://arxiv.org/html/2205.13698v2). Introduction, assumptions and motivating example inspected. Misspecified model classes can interact with active sampling to harm generalization. Their stated generative conditional is fixed in the inspected setup. We must not claim the general failure mode as a discovery; temporal-shift stress tests remain only a proposed evaluation extension.
- **Metrics for Bayesian Optimal Experiment Design under Model Misspecification**, Tommie A. Catanach, Niladri Das, 2023. [Record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.07949). Abstract screened. Proposed criteria explicitly address model discrepancy and its detection. A discrepancy-sensitive acquisition rule is already prior art. Full derivations and implementation are not reviewed here.
- **Representative, Informative, and De-Amplifying: Requirements for Robust Bayesian Active Learning under Model Misspecification**, Roubing Tang, Sabina J. Sloman, Samuel Kaski, AISTATS 2026. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v300/tang26d.html). Official abstract and metadata inspected; the linked PDF failed retrieval with an unsupported content-type error. R-IDeA addresses informativeness, representativeness and misspecification-related error amplification. This is a particularly close recent competitor: no robust-design novelty claim is warranted without a methods-level comparison. No numerical claims adopted.
- **Tackling Non-Stationarity in Reinforcement Learning via Causal-Origin Representation**, Wanpeng Zhang, Yilin Li, Boyu Yang, Zongqing Lu, ICML 2024. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/zhang24ah.html). Abstract screened. COREP already learns graph-related representations for nonstationary RL. Representation adaptation is an occupied area, and introducing neural latents tomorrow would add substantial confounds.
- **Challenging Common Assumptions in the Unsupervised Learning of Disentangled Representations**, Francesco Locatello et al., ICML 2019 (2018 preprint). [Record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.12359). Abstract screened. Identifiability requires assumptions/inductive biases in the setting studied; this is not a blanket impossibility theorem for all causal representation methods. Any future concept-discovery experiment needs an explicit observation model and identifying information.

For direct diagnostic-action collisions, see [independent critique](critique.md): controlled sensing and bandit quickest detection already study action-dependent observations, exploration and detection delay. The primary records [2310.17223](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.17223) and [2603.28563](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28563) were also opened by the primary agent.
