What did it predict?

Four categories mean an accepted transition to the pictured state. The fifth means blocked, with no visible state change. This outcome support is supplied to the learner.

Where did it expect to learn?

Greedy scores immediate predictive information. Lookahead scores immediate information + expected best next-action information in the predicted reachable state, including hypothetical belief updates. It replans after each actual observation; the second action is not committed. These totals are not environmental rewards. This is shallow model-based coverage planning: in deterministic worlds, scores depend on row visit counts. It does not establish an advantage of the information principle itself.

Full-study overview · includes future

All 32 seeds per world, independent of the replay cursor. Each cell is mean one-step excess-loss area over observations 1–96 / mean final two-step excess loss. Fixed cycle is descriptive only, not part of the primary comparison. Shared seeds and deterministic repeated paths are not independent world replications.

This learner estimates transition probabilities with fixed visible-state tables. It does not learn a neural representation or invent rules. Independent queries are evaluator calculations and never supply training outcomes or reset the actual room. A hidden-state control deliberately violates the visible Markov assumption; its evaluator reference is not the learner’s on-policy hidden-state conditional. Low error is not a calibration or novelty guarantee.
