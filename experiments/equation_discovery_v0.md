# Equation discovery v0 — pre-experiment protocol, September 26, 2026

## Direction and scope
The target is explicit, testable equations from observations. This first step is best-subset polynomial regression, not autonomous invention. Static two-input systems replace the rooms temporarily; dynamics, hidden-state discovery and the previously proposed memory extension are deferred. The supplied basis is `[1,x,u,x*x,x*u,u*u]`. Search all42supports of size0..3, including the zero equation, fit real coefficients and select structure by known-noise information criterion `SSE/sigma² + k*log(n)`. This is BIC-like scoring with known supplied Gaussian noise sigma=.05, not a calibrated posterior or original algorithm. Normalize exp(-.5*(score-min)) to heuristic model weights; save top3formulas/weights, full-precision coefficients, selected support, fit SSE and complexity. Score ties within1e-12of the minimum choose fewest terms, then lexicographically smallest support. No exact-formula menu or true support is passed to the learner. Variables, monomials, maximum size and noise model are supplied.

## Baselines, solver and audit
All methods get identical24observations: mean-only constant; full affine3terms; full dense quadratic6terms; sparse subset selection0..3. Stable least-squares via modified Gram-Schmidt QR with a second orthogonalization pass; skip a support if any twice-orthogonalized column norm<=1e-10times its original column norm (zerooriginalnorm also fails). Empty support predicts0. The dense/affine baseline must report failure if singular, never silently replace itself. Any of the four methods failing numerically invalidates full-run completeness and both criteria; failed baselines are not dropped. No ridge strength tuning or downloads. Synthetic rank/polynomial fixtures test the solver independently.

Independent audit rejection flag: average of16squared predictive residuals >4*sigma²=.01. This is an engineering adequacy threshold including estimation error, not a calibrated p-value/F-test or proof of correctness. Report false rejects on in-class/noise and failures to reject on outsidegrammar. A passed audit means only 'not rejected at this sample size and domain', never 'true law'. Replicates remain available to expose noise estimation but noise scale is supplied; no estimated-noise adaptation thisstage. Known-scale audit avoids claiming a classical F-test is valid after structure selection.

Claude estimates noise-only nonzero selection around20–40%; this is a rough, unverified heuristic, not a theoretical guarantee or a test assertion. Sampling variation and correlations matter. These controls test a knownmethod, notnovelty. Shared input/noise makes familygates correlated; do not multiply their evidential significance.

Population projections under uniform[-1,1] suggest sin(pi*x)+.5u retains about.196latentMSE afterbestquadratic fitting; exponential+.5u has about.026afterbest3termfit butabout.0007afterdensequadratic. Its practical diagnostic objection is valid: sparse rejection can be dominated by the term-count cap, whereas dense polynomial approximation can hide the functional mismatch locally.
