## One immutable scientific record

Log A selection separately from the reported refitted equation, exact support/coefficients/formula, fitted-data designation and frozen C predictions. Store SHA256 fingerprints of the candidate and predictions before/after C checks and fail on mutation. Compact selection logs may retain the chosen candidate and top3; all42 support fits remain defined by the unchanged fitter. A learner function takes only observed A/B; another takes that frozen candidate plus C observations and known settings. No hidden means/supports enter either function. The C object records:

1. Whole-form adequacy: construct QR of the fitted design restricted to candidate support S. V=sigma²[I16+A_C,S*(X_fit,S^T X_fit,S)^(-1)*A_C,S^T]. Empty S gives sigma²I. Q=r^T V^-1 r, r=y_C−prediction. Reuse stable df16 log survival and Cholesky helpers; reject log_p<=log(.05). For selected_refit, conditional on A/selected support and B/C designs, Q is central chi-square16 only under the null that the true mean belongs to that selected span; marginalize over BOTH B and C noise. Omitted weak terms make the null false. The fixed_full baseline has the ordinary fixed-full-family null. For pooled_bic, do not calculate or label a chi-square adequacy verdict: report status unavailable_post_selection with Q/p/rejection/qualified flag null. Its predictions and independent term evidence are valid comparator outputs; no exact post-selection whole-form test is claimed. No conditioning on realized fitted coefficients.
2. Term evidence: unchanged six full-five-term null likelihood fits on C, known variance, candidate frozen from independent data. log E_j=(SSE_null_j−SSE_candidate)/(2sigma²); claim selected terms only at log E>=log120. The full six-polynomial Gaussian family gives per-pipeline per-dataset false-term family-wise bound<=.05; this is a conservative bound, not exact size, and is not advertised simultaneously over all pipelines/worlds. Outside that family no guarantee. These tests and Q share C; do not multiply evidence or assume independence.
3. Report the unmodified equation, supported and unsupported selected terms, adequacy verdict, and a qualified nonempty flag: 'adequacy not rejected; every selected term confirmed'. Never call it a true law or evidence that no terms are missing. Empty support remains an abstention for term claims while retaining its adequacy result. The full model selects all6basis terms, including true-zero terms in these worlds; its all-selected-confirmed flag will consequently be disadvantaged. Flag differences do not establish structural or predictive superiority; show supported and unsupported terms separately.
