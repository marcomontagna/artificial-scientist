# Adaptive context v2 — fast simple alternative and reversible choice

Status: protocol accepted by distinct Codex research_critic before implementation, with its clarifications below. Exploratory follow-up to v1, informed by its failures; no novelty, causal-diagnosis, calibrated-test or efficiency claim. Existing [prior-art audit](../research/state_revision_prior_art.md) applies. Keep v1 and its results unchanged.

## Fixed design
Bank of six discounted Beta(1,1) experts: lag1 with discounts .99 and .90 (slow/fast), then sparse lags(1,k), k2..5, discount .99. All learn from each observed outcome; prediction precedes update. The .90 setting is a fixed engineering choice, not optimized. Maintain six trailing128 pre-update log-loss buffers. Start with slow lag1. Every64 observations after the first128, choose the simple expert (slow/fast) with lowest window loss. Choose the best sparse expert only if its window loss is more than log400 lower than that best simple expert. Otherwise choose that simple expert. Ties choose the lower bank index. Decisions affect the next prediction. Selection is reversible: slow/fast swap, expansion, contraction or sparse lag reselection. No hidden labels, initialization, lag or change time enter the learner.

All shadows remain trained and allocated. Logical slots include every model's key/count/history/lag-index storage, six128-capacity loss buffers and six selector scalars; Python object overhead and temporaries excluded. Active feature count is not memory saved. Measure predict/update time separately; tiny single-run timings do not prove speed differences.

Four structural variants explicitly use lag2/3/4/5 for each seed, giving20 structural worlds and35 total. Reuse v0 hidden initialization/noise streams and rule function; only force structural lag. Verify existing random-lag worlds reproduce when their lag is forced. Same prefix and noise uniforms across conditions; these are correlated cases, not35 independent replications. Labels/true lag remain evaluator-only. Reserved seeds1000..1049 stay unused; there is no held-out or unseen-family claim.

Six competitors: slow lag1, fast lag1, unchanged v1, original five-expert sparse mixture, matched six-expert fixed-share(.01) mixture, v2. Use identical banks/settings between v2 and matched mixture. All receive the same realized stream. No threshold/discount sweep or change after viewing results. 252000 predictions; standard-library only, one process, <10 minutes,<2GB,<500MB.
