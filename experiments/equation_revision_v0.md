# Reject, revise, and check afresh — protocol v0

**Presampling protocol, reviewed independently and amended after Claude’s critique. Implementation requires coordinator approval recorded in the review log; full sampling requires code review and the fixed smoke gate.** This is a bounded integration of known regression/model-selection methods, not rule invention. Question: can one evidence-triggered expansion of a supplied grammar improve common-grid prediction over never revising, without losing to an always-expanded model? Passing a validity check alone cannot answer that question.

## Worlds, information, and budget

Use 60 new development seeds 1200–1259, smoke 1190, synthetic fixtures 7/11. Preserve reserve 1000–1049. Known independent Gaussian observation noise has sigma=0.05. Base features are [1,x,u,x²,xu,u²]; optional supplied features are x³ and u³. Initial learner sees only the base grammar. Revision may add exactly one of the two supplied cubic features; no invented operator or second revision.

Eight fixed world types share designs/noise within a seed. Let q be the unchanged stage1 generated sparse quadratic (at most three nonzero terms): quadratic q; noise zero; weak_x q+0.01x³; medium_x q+0.05x³; strong_x q+0.3x³; medium_u q+0.05u³; both q+0.05x³+0.05u³; exponential q+0.3exp(x). Hidden coefficients, missing-term identities and sufficiency labels are evaluator-only. Both-cubic tests the one-extension limit; exponential lies outside the full eight-feature family. These are graded fixed alternatives, not independent new function families.

Four disjoint observed datasets: A24 at 24 independently generated uniform sites in [-1,1]²; C16 uniform sites in [-2,2]²; B24 uniform sites in [-2,2]²; D24 uniform sites in [-2,2]². Total 88 observations per world, regardless of initial decision; 480 datasets cost 42,240 observations shared across five pipelines. B is deliberately wide, avoiding an unnecessary local-refit extrapolation penalty. All locations are fixed independently of outcomes. No acquisition policy yet.

Use Random(offset+seed): law offset 9100001; A-input/noise 13100001/13200003, C-input/noise 13300007/13400009, B-input/noise 13500011/13600013, D-input/noise 13700017/13800019. Before sampling assert uniqueness of every stream key across study/smoke/fixture seeds. There must be 567 unique keys (9 roles × 63 study/smoke/fixture seeds). A, B and D each use 24 independently generated sites, with no deliberate replication. Within-seed pairing is intentional. Do not modify historical sources or regenerate earlier results.

## Frozen decision sequence and comparators

1. Fit all six base terms on A. Predict C before seeing C outcomes; apply the known-noise predictive whole-form test below at alpha=0.025. Log equation, predictions, Q and decision. C rejection is the only revision trigger.
2. If rejected, fit the base plus x³ and the base plus u³ on A+C, choosing lower SSE (equivalently known-sigma BIC, since both have seven parameters; exact/numerical ties within 1e-12 choose x³). If not rejected retain the base support. This selection may reuse C because neither C nor its outcomes will supply final confirmation. Do not call C fresh evidence for the chosen revision.
3. Refit the chosen support using B alone, freeze equation and D predictions, then inspect D. Even without revision, refit on B; any improvement therefore needs the matched never-revise comparison. Final rejection means unresolved: no further selection, rollback, refit, or sampling.

Five final pipelines: **revise_once** as above; **never_revise** six fixed terms fitted on B24; **always_large** all eight terms fitted on B24; **always_large_pooled** all eight terms fitted on A+C+B64 without selection; **pooled_bic** ordinary known-sigma BIC over four supplied supports (base6, base6+x³, base6+u³, full8), fitted on A+C+B64. All receive the same total observation budget. The fixed baselines deliberately do not use the earlier 40 observations for coefficient estimation, isolating the effect of support revision on matched B data; they are not the most sample-efficient implementations of fixed models. The pooled full model and pooled BIC are the efficient allocation comparators. The fixed pooled full model has an unconditional fixed-span predictive Q using all64 fit observations and D24; do not interpret it conditional on A+C outcomes. Only pooled BIC’s final whole-form p-value is unavailable after same-data selection, but independent term evidence and predictions remain available. Report this allocation difference prominently; do not infer general superiority from beating an artificially split fixed baseline.

## Final evidence and error families

For a fixed support S fitted on independent training set T, let r=y_E-X_E,S beta_hat and V=sigma²[I+X_E,S(X_T,SᵀX_T,S)^(-1)X_E,Sᵀ]. Use QR/Cholesky, not a naive inverse. Q=rᵀV^(-1)r is chi-square with |E| degrees of freedom when the true mean lies in S, conditional on selection/designs but marginal over training and audit noise. Initial Q uses T=A,E=C, df16. Final Q uses T=B,E=D, df24, except always_large_pooled uses fixed T=A+C+B,E=D, also df24. Conditional on A+C selection, B and D remain independent. Both-cubic and exponential can violate the final span null. Pooled BIC has no post-selection Q. Any rank failure/nonfinite output invalidates the whole study, without resampling.

Reject initial and final Q at 0.025 each. Their union bound limits erroneous rejection of true span-null hypotheses along this two-check sequence to 0.05; it does not bound intended rejection of false hypotheses or certify an accepted formula. Fixed baselines use final alpha=0.025. Implement survival for both even degrees: log[e^(-Q/2) sum from k=0 to df/2-1 of (Q/2)^k/k!], with stable log arithmetic, Q=0 handled exactly. Do not accidentally reuse df16 for D24.

On untouched D, calculate two distinct coefficient-evidence outputs for every pipeline:

- **Exact coefficient tests:** fit the full eight-feature regression on D. For each of eight coefficients, z=beta_hat_j/[sigma sqrt((X_DᵀX_D)^(-1)_jj)], two-sided p=erfc(|z|/sqrt(2)). Bonferroni threshold 0.05/8. Log all eight tests and their selected-support intersection. They concern population coefficients in the supplied full family, not the accuracy of the candidate's fitted coefficient. They are the same D-based tests across pipelines, not independent confirmations.
- **Candidate likelihood evidence:** freeze candidate from pre-D data, fit each seven-feature zero-coefficient null on D, and calculate log E_j=(SSE_null_j-SSE_candidate)/(2sigma²). Eight-test threshold log E >= log(160) (approximately5.07517); never compare a log statistic with160. Report selected terms meeting it. This concerns evidence relative to a candidate's predictive density. In this particular fixed-variance linear family, candidate SSE >= full-eight D-fit SSE, hence log E_j <= z_j²/2. E-confirmation implies two-sided exact p <= erfc(sqrt(log160)) approximately0.00144274 <0.00625. Therefore exact-z selected claims contain E-claims here; this is an algebraic consequence, not an empirical novelty or a general comparison for other model families.

Each coefficient method has its own per-dataset false-term family bound <=0.05 under the full eight-feature Gaussian family. The two methods share D with Q. Never union their reported claims, multiply their evidence, or advertise a joint 0.05 guarantee across methods, pipelines, worlds and Q. Exponential has no coefficient-null guarantee. Zero observed false claims is not proof of calibration. Report unsupported selected terms; neither all-confirmed nor Q nonrejection establishes no missing terms.

## Evaluation and prespecified decision

Report every seed/world/pipeline: initial rejection and selected extension; final support/equation; raw/confirmed false-term counts by evidence method; all-true-term and selected-true-term confirmation denominators separately; exact-support match; final Q/rejection or unavailable status; evaluator support sufficiency; D MSE; common fixed stage1 inner100/outer-shell56-grid latent MSE; fit allocation, observations and timing. Report pooled confirmed-true/all-true term recall and mean per-dataset ratios separately; exclude empty denominators from the latter and do not multiply averages. Hidden truth enters only after all candidate/claim objects are frozen. Pure-noise true-term recall and exponential truth-support metrics are null. The old grid helper calls its56point shell "extrapolation", but it lies within the B/D domain; use outer_shell_mse in new artifacts and do not claim extrapolation beyond [-2,2]². Common-grid prediction, not candidate-specific evidence magnitude, determines utility.

Hash the initial equation and C predictions before/after its check, and every final candidate and D predictions before/after final checks; changes invalidate the run. Check log E_j <= z_j²/2 +1e-8 and E-confirmed terms are a subset of the exact-selected claims. For a term outside the candidate support, assert log E_j <= 1e-8 because the null likelihood supremum includes the candidate.

Hard integration gate: all 480 datasets/2,400 final candidates, immutable hashes, expected budgets, finite/rank-valid calculations, source provenance and tests. Verify the initial base-Q control statistics for quadratic/noise agree under shared design/noise up to absolute 1e-8 plus relative 1e-10; these are duplicate null checks, not 120 independent trials. Do not assert such identity for selected-candidate coefficient evidence.

Separate descriptive utility screen, allowed to fail: in EACH of medium_x and strong_x, revise_once mean outer-shell MSE <=0.8 times never_revise and <=always_large and <=always_large_pooled; in EACH quadratic/noise control, mean outer-shell MSE <=1.10 times never_revise. The two control residual/prediction-error comparisons duplicate the same noise experiment; list both but count one effective control. Report initial false revisions in each control; >6/60 triggers a review warning, not a conclusion that the theorem is false. This warning is recorded separately and does not change utility_screen_pass; hard validity, descriptive utility and warning flags are distinct. Report pooled-BIC comparisons even when they defeat revision. Both-cubic, weak_x, medium_u and exponential characterize limits and never get silently dropped. No empirical calibration gate or novelty claim follows from utility passing. All thresholds are engineering targets, not predicted power or significance bounds. Failure against the efficient pooled full model is an informative no-go for a superiority claim; do not weaken that gate after outcomes. The fixed-design analytic feasibility note documents expected bias/variance and trigger-power limitations before sampling; it does not integrate over the random design distribution or predict selection probabilities exactly.

Provide seed-paired differences and descriptive intervals separately per world, plus Wilson intervals for rates; no pooled duplicate controls or independent-family claims. A fixed budget supports a utility comparison, not observations saved. No choosing seeds, coefficients, domains, thresholds or extra revisions after seeing results.
